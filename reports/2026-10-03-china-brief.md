## 🚩 Top Flags (read first)

1. **NEW — China opens first overseas *airpower* base, in Laos (Sep 29–30).** The **China–Laos Military Joint Support and Training Center at Ban Keun Airport** began operations Sep 29; PRC MOD frames it as pilot-training support, jointly maintained. ~6,560 ft runway, parallel taxiway, apron, 5 hangars, built over the past year on former farmland. This is China's **third acknowledged overseas facility** (after Djibouti naval, Cambodia Ream naval) and the **first centered on air power**. **India implication: HIGH** — a PLAAF-linked foothold on mainland Southeast Asia, astride the Mekong and on India's eastern approaches, extends Beijing's continental reach and complicates the Act East / BIMSTEC security picture.
2. **National Defense Mobilization Law (revised) took effect Oct 1.** Adopted by the NPC Standing Committee Aug 28; now explicitly covers **civilian cutting-edge tech (drones, AI)**, codifies peacetime→wartime transition procedures and peacetime supply-chain resilience checks, and allows mobilization for "development interests," not just sovereignty. Analysts read it as full-resource war-preparation (Taiwan in mind); MOD's Jiang Bin calls it routine and warns against over-interpretation.
3. **Fifth Plenum locked for Oct 26–29, Beijing** (set by Sep 21 Politburo). Theme is **Party self-governance** (NOT the 15th Five-Year Plan — that was the Oct 2025 *Fourth* Plenum). ~300+ full/alternate CC members. Last major conclave before the 2027 Party Congress reshuffle; watch for discipline/personnel signals after the 2025 CMC purges.
4. **AI-hardware:** DeepSeek/Huawei have **open-sourced Ascend programming tooling (TileLang)** as a CUDA alternative (WeChat announcement), reinforcing the ~160k **Ascend 950DT** Inner Mongolia build-out and a possible **training** expansion; Huawei reportedly pulled the **Ascend 960DT forward to 1Q27**. Chips/stack decoupling is accelerating.

## 1. Defense Tech & Platforms

- **J-35 "Blue Shark" toward mass deployment; J-35AE export push.** Carrier-based J-35 (PLAN/PLAAF service since Sep 2025, 57+ built by Oct 2025) now officially nicknamed "Blue Shark"; land-based variant "Cloud Dragon"; **J-35AE** marketed as a lower-cost, land-optimized F-35 alternative "as new buyers emerge" (Army Recognition, 2026). WS-21 engines, ~Mach 1.8, ~1,200 km combat radius (1,500 km refueled). *India implication:* a credible fifth-gen export jet in the neighborhood — Pakistan the obvious prospective operator — pressures the IAF's fifth-gen timeline (AMCA) and air-dominance calculus. (Snippet-derived; no J-35 hand-over to Pakistan confirmed.)
- **SLBM activity.** A July 6, 2026 PLAN SLBM test (likely **JL-2/JL-3**) from an SSBN into the South Pacific remains the standing sea-based-deterrent data-point. (Carried; no new launch this pass.)

## 2. Drones / Autonomy & Military AI

- **"Atlas" drone-swarm system** (first full-process state-media demo Mar 25, 2026): coordinated reconnaissance → command-vehicle ID → launch → in-flight target-lock precision strike — i.e., an operational kill-chain, not a concept. **"Jiu Tian" ("High Sky") drone mothership**, ~10-ton MTOW, built to launch mid-air swarms. (Carried/background; no new Oct event.)

(Western-reported; unverified independently.)

## 3. AI / Semiconductors
